Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our London domestic, deep, and commercial cleaning services.

Do I need to be at home during the cleaning appointment? expand_more
No, over 70% of our regular clients provide key access or use our secure keyholding protocol. Our staff are fully insured, enhanced DBS checked, and strictly vetted so you can return to an immaculate home without disrupting your schedule.
What is included in Domestic Cleaning vs Deep Cleaning? expand_more
Regular domestic cleaning covers everyday upkeep, surfaces, dusting, sanitary sanitisation, hoovering, mopping, and ironing. Deep cleaning includes full inside-appliance degreasing (ovens, fridges), limescale extraction, behind-furniture clearance, woodwork detailing, and tile scrubbing.
Are cleaning materials and equipment provided? expand_more
Yes! For one-off deep cleans, end-of-tenancy, and specialist carpet services we bring all commercial-grade eco-friendly chemicals, steam wands, and Numatic Henry vacuums. For weekly domestic visits, we can use your preferred products or supply our luxury eco-friendly kit.
Are your cleaners insured and vetted? expand_more
Absolutely. All Super Cleaners London professionals undergo rigorous background verification, criminal record checks (Enhanced DBS), identity screening, and in-house five-star standard training. In addition, we hold £10,000,000 public liability insurance.
How do cancellations or rescheduling work? expand_more
We offer ultimate flexibility with zero lock-in contracts. As long as you give us at least 24 hours notice, you can reschedule or cancel your cleaning visit via WhatsApp or phone call without any penalty fees.
How do I get an exact quote or book? expand_more
You can click 'Book Online Now', send quick room photos via our WhatsApp concierge (07786 282887), or call our London desk directly at 020 3664 9304 for an immediate, transparent estimate.
