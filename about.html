A trusted, family-run cleaning company, dedicated to exceptional service, high standards and long-term customer care.

Super Cleaners London was founded in 2006 by Myriam Andrade, a Peruvian Latina, with a passion for cleaning and a clear goal: to provide a reliable, personal and high-quality service for homes and businesses across London.

We are a family-run company with a dedicated team of Latina women. We provide a complete range of cleaning services, including Domestic Cleaning Services for homes, Commercial and other Professional Cleaning Services for businesses and offices, and specialist Carpet and Upholstery Cleaning Services to keep your spaces fresh, clean and well maintained.

We may not have millions of customers, but we do have hundreds of happy, loyal customers who trust us and value our work. We keep in touch with them to ensure our high standards are consistently maintained.

We believe in simple, friendly and straightforward communication — no complicated contracts. We are flexible and happy to adjust and adapt to each customer's individual needs, whether it's a regular service or a one-off clean.

Myriam, our Founder and CEO, personally handles all customer service enquiries and is always available and happy to help and assist. We take great pride in what we do, and our goal is to continue delivering a professional, reliable and caring service, with outstanding results every time.
