Frequently Asked Pricing Questions

Direct answers about billing, deposits, London parking charges, and cancellations.

For Regular Domestic cleaning, clients typically provide standard detergents and vacuums to prevent cross-home contamination. However, your cleaner can provide high-grade eco-detergents and ProTeam HEPA vacuum units for a flat £3.00/hour supplement. For all Deep Cleans and End of Tenancy services, all heavy-duty chemicals and industrial machinery are 100% included in the quoted fee.
We provide a strict 72-hour re-clean guarantee. If your landlord or inventory clerk notes any specific discrepancies against our letting agent checklist within 72 hours of completion, our supervisor returns to resolve them completely free of charge.
For Regular Domestic housekeeping, our staff travel via London Underground and public transport, incurring zero parking surcharges. For heavy-duty Deep Cleans and Tenancy vans carrying equipment, we absorb the ULEZ charge; parking scratch-cards or visitor permits provided by the resident are preferred, or actual street meter costs will be pre-agreed upfront with zero surcharge markup.
We accept all major Visa/Mastercard/Amex cards, automated direct debits, and BACS bank transfers for corporate accounts. No prepayment deposit is required for recurring domestic cleans; fees are settled post-clean. For one-off services, you can cancel or reschedule without penalty up to 24 hours prior to appointment dispatch.
