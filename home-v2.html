Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We’ve Got Answers

Everything you need to know about our London cleaning services

Do I need to be at home during the cleaning appointment? expand_more

No, many of our clients provide key access or use our secure keyholding service. Our staff are fully insured, trustworthy, and respectful, giving you total peace of mind while you work or enjoy your day.

What is included in your Domestic Cleaning vs Deep Cleaning? expand_more

Regular domestic cleaning covers everyday upkeep, dusting, kitchen sanitisation, hoovering, mopping, and ironing. Deep cleaning includes full inside-appliance degreasing, limescale removal, behind-furniture extraction, and woodwork scrub following strict inventory checklists.

How does your pricing work, and is oven cleaning included? expand_more

We clean from top to bottom with clear pricing and no hidden fees. Additional specialist services such as oven cleaning are charged separately per item, allowing you to tailor your booking exactly to your requirements.

Are cleaning materials and equipment provided? expand_more

Yes, for one-off deep cleans, end of tenancy, and specialist services we bring all commercial-grade, high-quality cleaning equipment and professional products. For regular domestic visits, we can use your preferred supplies or provide our professional kit.

Are your cleaners fully trained and insured? expand_more

All Super Cleaners London cleaners undergo thorough background checks, in-person interviews, and continuous professional training and supervision. We hold £10,000,000 in comprehensive public liability insurance for complete homeowner and business protection.

How do cancellations or rescheduling work? expand_more

We offer flexible scheduling. Just give us at least 24 hours notice to reschedule or cancel without any penalty.

How do I get an exact quote or book? expand_more

You can click 'Book Now', message us on WhatsApp (07786 282887 or 07507 00167), or call our London desk directly at 020 3664 9304 for an instant estimate.
