Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We’ve Got Answers

Everything you need to know about our London cleaning services

Do I need to be at home during the cleaning appointment? expand_more

No, over 70% of our clients provide key access or use our secure keyholding protocol. Our staff are fully insured and DBS vetted, giving you total peace of mind while you work or enjoy your day.

What is included in your Domestic Cleaning vs Deep Cleaning? expand_more

Regular domestic cleaning covers everyday upkeep, dusting, kitchen sanitisation, hoovering, mopping, and ironing. Deep cleaning includes full inside-appliance degreasing, limescale removal, behind-furniture extraction, and woodwork scrub following strict inventory checklists.

Are cleaning materials and equipment provided? expand_more

Yes, for one-off deep cleans, end of tenancy, and specialist services we bring all commercial-grade eco-friendly chemicals and Prochem/Henry vacuums. For regular domestic visits, we can use your preferred supplies or provide our luxury eco kit.

Are your cleaners insured and vetted? expand_more

All Super Cleaners London cleaners undergo strict background checks, enhanced DBS checks, in-person interviews, and continuous hotel-grade training. We hold £10,000,000 in public liability insurance for complete homeowner protection.

How do cancellations or rescheduling work? expand_more

We offer flexible scheduling. Just give us at least 24 hours notice to reschedule or cancel without any penalty.

How do I get an exact quote or book? expand_more

You can click 'Book Now', send us photos via WhatsApp, or call our London desk directly at 020 3664 9304 for an instant estimate.
