General & Booking

Key access, minimum hours, areas covered & communication

Do I need to be at home during the cleaning appointment? expand_more

No, many of our clients provide key access or use our secure keyholding arrangement. Our staff are vetted, uniformed, fully insured, and respectful, giving you total peace of mind while you work or enjoy your day.

What is your minimum booking duration and how does pricing work? expand_more

Our cleaning services start from a minimum of 3 hours, with clear, transparent hourly or tailored quotes and no hidden fees. Specialist items such as deep oven cleaning are priced separately per item so you only pay for what you need.

Which areas of London do you cover? expand_more

For regular domestic and housekeeping services, our core hub covers Fulham, Parsons Green, Sands End, Imperial Wharf, Chelsea, Putney, Earls Court, Earlsfield, Wandsworth, West Brompton, Clapham, Hammersmith, South Kensington, Battersea and Chiswick. For professional cleaning services (end-of-tenancy, deep, after-builders, move-in/out and carpet steam cleaning), we cover all areas across London.

Are your cleaners trained and insured, and what languages do you speak? expand_more

Yes — our dedicated team of Latina women are vetted, trained, uniformed, supervised and covered by comprehensive insurance. We speak English, Spanish and Portuguese, and Myriam (our Founder & CEO) personally handles all customer enquiries.

Professional & Deep Cleaning

One-off, deep, end-of-tenancy, after-builders & move-in/out cleans

View Service arrow_forward
What is the difference between Domestic Cleaning and Deep Cleaning? expand_more

Regular domestic cleaning maintains your home week-to-week (surfaces, bathrooms, kitchens, floors and tidying). Deep cleaning is an intensive, top-to-bottom service targeting limescale, mould, built-up grease, skirting boards, grout lines and hard-to-reach areas.

Does End-of-Tenancy Cleaning meet landlord and letting agent standards? expand_more

Yes — our end-of-tenancy cleaning is designed to meet strict landlord and letting agent inventory standards, helping you hand over the property in excellent condition. We can also include same-day carpet steam cleaning alongside your end-of-tenancy clean.

Do you provide cleaning products, tools and equipment? expand_more

Yes! For professional cleans (deep, end-of-tenancy, after-builders, move-in/out and one-off visits), we can supply all cleaning products, tools and professional equipment, or you are welcome to supply your own if you prefer specific products.
