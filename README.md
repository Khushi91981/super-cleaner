# Super Cleaners London

This repository contains the client-approved, enhanced production website for Super Cleaners London.

## Primary Live Implementation (Approved Version 2 Direction)

Following client feedback:
- **Client-Approved Direction**: Version 2 layout, typography, section styling, spacing, and visual identity are approved as the primary website design.
- **Cinematic Video Hero**: Incorporates Version 1's video background concept with a lighter blue tint overlay (keeping people and cleaning activity clearly visible without looking dark or black), centered high-resolution diamond SC logo, centered headings, and a standout red "Book Your Clean Today" CTA button.
- **Streamlined Navigation**: Clean top navigation displaying **Home**, **Services**, **FAQ**, **About Us**, and **Contact**, with direct telephone contact (`020 3664 9304`), dual WhatsApp direct lines (`07786 282887` & `07507 00167`), and prominent red **Book Now** booking action.
- **Accurate Brand Claims**: Removed unverified promotional claims (e.g. "Eco-friendly", "5-star hotel protocol", "100% DBS vetted", "18,500+ homes cleaned").
- **Updated Feature Cards**:
  1. *Trusted & Professional Team* — Experienced cleaners you can trust in your home.
  2. *Fully Trained & Insured* — Professional cleaners who are fully trained, uniformed and supervised.
  3. *Flexible, Quick Bookings* — Bookings tailored to your needs, with fast booking by WhatsApp or online.
  4. *Clear Pricing, No Hidden Fees* — Straightforward cleaning services with clear pricing and no hidden fees (with oven cleaning charged separately per item).
- **Core Services Alignment**: Domestic Cleaning & Housekeeping, Professional Cleaning Services (one-off, deep, end-of-tenancy, after-build), Commercial Cleaning Services (offices, clinics, salons, retail, gyms, warehouses, workshops), and Carpet & Upholstery Cleaning.
- **Team Showcase**: Features the real Super Cleaners London team in branded blue uniforms (`images/team.jpg`).
